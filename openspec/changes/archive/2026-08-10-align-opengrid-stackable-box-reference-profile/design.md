## Context

The existing stackable-box implementation already has separated geometry and quality modules, but its constants and profile builders describe the earlier thick-shell design. The new capability delta is based on the measured side profile of `public/box-2-2-3.step`; it must retain the local 28 mm/half-cell footprint and hole contracts while changing the vertical datum and mating geometry.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Use a fixed Z=0 bottom datum and a fixed 5.0 mm bottom assembly, with the user-provided `height` representing clear interior height.
- Reproduce the reference upper rail and sliding-block sequence with fixed 45° transitions and a fused one-solid rim.
- Reproduce the reference bottom guide layout at the local 28 mm cell boundaries while making the 5 mm lower assembly printable from the bed upward.
- Preserve hole centers, the 14 mm nominal grid, half-cell footprints, Snap retention, sliding, bridging, and export behavior.
- Add geometry evidence for derived external height, reference profile segments, bottom support, and the continuous floor retained above each relief.

**Non-Goals:**

- Scaling the reference model's overall 42 mm-style footprint or copying its hole locations.
- Adding user controls for wall, floor, rail, guide, or clearance dimensions.
- Maintaining geometric mating compatibility with solids generated by the previous profile.
- Changing the separate official OpenGrid generator or OpenGrid Snap model.

## Decisions

### 1. Treat the input height as clear interior height

Use the upper surface of the fixed 5.0 mm bottom assembly as the lower interior-height datum. Place the upper inner-rim datum exactly `height` millimetres above it, then attach the fixed upper profile above that datum. The nominal external height is therefore `height + 5.0 + 7.55` millimetres, with only the reference top-edge rounding excluded from the arithmetic contract.

This preserves intuitive usable-space semantics and avoids forcing the minimum input height to include the fixed stacking interface. The footprint remains centered and unchanged by the height conversion.

**Alternative rejected:** Continuing to use `height` as the outer Z bound would make the fixed reference profiles overlap at common heights and would change the usable interior space whenever the interface is revised.

### 2. Encode the reference upper profile as one canonical staircase

Build the top rim as a rounded ring fused into the nominal 1.2 mm side wall, using the reference 3.75 mm external corner radius and a compact 0.8 mm inner rail corner radius, then trim the inside of the ring with one canonical side profile. On each side, the profile uses this sequence from the inner-rim datum upward and outward:

```text
1.75 mm / 45° inner lead-in
1.20 mm vertical sliding block
0.80 mm / 45° transition
1.80 mm vertical segment
2.00 mm / 45° return to the outer wall
```

Keep the ring within the derived outer envelope and retain a positive bearing land. The small reference top rounding may remain a finishing tolerance and must not become a separate mating datum.

**Alternative rejected:** Applying generic chamfers to a flat rim would not preserve the sliding-block height or the complementary bottom receiving profile and would make the fit dependent on edge-selection behavior.

### 3. Build the bottom as a supported 5 mm assembly

Use the following fixed vertical decomposition:

```text
Z 0.0–0.8   bed-facing 45° foot chamfer
Z 0.8–2.6   vertical guide/support segment
Z 2.6–3.8   45° guide transition into the floor
Z 3.8–5.0   supported 1.2 mm interior floor
```

The two sloped segments remain 45° by reducing both their vertical and horizontal run where the reference transition is shortened for the 5 mm total. The bottom guide is generated along the outer and internal cell-boundary pattern needed for self-mating, not as a separate floating plate.

The floor core and guide transitions must be fused into continuous solid material. A boolean cut may open a relief from the bed side, but it must stop at the lower surface of the supported floor at Z=3.8 and must not cut through to the box interior. The horizontal underside at that datum is part of the supported floor core, not a separate suspended plate; it is measured directly above every relief.

The lower profile has no additional user-adjustable dimensions: the outer support inset is the fixed 1.2 mm transition run, and the internal seam openings are derived from the same fixed profile as 5.6 mm at the bed, 4.0 mm through the vertical support, and 1.6 mm at the floor underside.

**Alternative rejected:** Copying the reference's lower horizontal face as a separate underside plate would reproduce a potentially unsupported bridge. Copy the profile dimensions and guide layout, but close the transition into supported material.

### 4. Keep XY and hole contracts independent from the reference footprint

Continue deriving the footprint from `x × 28 − 0.15` and `y × 28 − 0.15`, centered at the origin, with half-cell support unchanged. Continue deriving nominal hole positions from the un-cleared 28 mm footprint and 14 mm hole grid. Apply the fixed corner socket stages after the structural bottom is formed: Ø5.05 mm through the lower/outside 3.0 mm and Ø7.05 mm through the upper/interior 2.0 mm, with the fixed retaining shoulder.

This keeps the reference profile dimensions in the stacking axis while preventing the reference model's overall size or hole layout from leaking into the OpenGrid contract.

### 5. Validate actual mating and printability

Extend the existing quality report and geometry gate rather than relying on bounds alone. Measure the derived external height and interior-height datum, main 1.2 mm wall and floor regions, the five upper profile segments, the three lower profile segments, the fused bearing land, and the 5 mm bottom assembly. Add positive probes for the bed-facing relief, the lower floor surface, and continuous supported material above every guide seam.

Retain translated self-mating fixtures for 1×1 over 1×4 sliding, the 2×2 bridge over two 1×2 boxes, half-cell footprints, corner sockets, full-hole mode, watertight B-Rep, mesh, STEP, and STL export.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **[The fixed interface increases external height relative to the input value]** → Treat `height` as clear interior height, assert the derived external bound explicitly, and update panel/docs wording.
- **[The 1.2 mm reference wall/floor is thinner than the previous thick-shell implementation]** → Keep the reference values fixed by contract, measure them outside intentional transitions, and reject unsupported candidates.
- **[Shortening the lower 45° transition can reduce lateral capture]** → Preserve the 45° angle, keep the upper rail dimensions fixed, and validate seated capture and continuous sliding with translated fixtures.
- **[Boolean operations can cut a relief through the floor]** → Build the supported floor core before relief cuts, stop the cutter at its lower surface, and probe material above every seam.
- **[Existing exported solids will no longer mate]** → Document the geometry break while keeping parameter payloads, model identity, hole coordinates, and export workflows stable.

## Migration Plan

1. Update the shared contract constants and height/bounds derivation.
2. Rebuild the shell, upper staircase, and supported bottom guide using the reference dimensions and fixed Z datums.
3. Reapply corner and ordinary hole cutters against the fixed 5 mm bottom assembly.
4. Update quality modules, error mapping, tests, panel/catalog/README descriptions, and OpenSpec-linked acceptance evidence.
5. Run targeted unit and Worker B-Rep tests, formatting/typecheck/build, strict OpenSpec validation, and the full relevant stackable-box flow.
